## Intuition

Two datasets can share a center and have very different spreads. The mean-centered deviation measures signed displacement; summing deviations gives zero and loses spread. Squaring keeps all distances nonnegative, and the square pictures make large deviations visibly influential.

## Definition

Population variance of these finite observations is $v_n=n^{-1}\sum_i(x_i-\bar x)^2$. Usual sample variance is $s^2=(n-1)^{-1}\sum_i(x_i-\bar x)^2$ for $n>1$. Their standard deviations are the nonnegative square roots. The unscaled median absolute deviation is $\operatorname{MAD}=\operatorname{median}_i|x_i-m|$, where $m$ is the observed median.

## Mechanics

Move or select observed values. Every square area is proportional to its displayed squared deviation; all squares share a scale within the dataset. The histogram, inspector and immutable replay use the same observations. The one-observation preset deliberately makes the usual sample variance undefined.

## Derivation

Expansion yields $\sum_i(x_i-\bar x)^2=\sum_i x_i^2-n\bar x^2$. For iid data with finite population variance $\sigma^2$, $E[\sum_i(X_i-\bar X)^2]=(n-1)\sigma^2$. Division by $n-1$ makes $s^2$ unbiased for that model; it does not imply that $s$ is an unbiased estimator of $\sigma$.

## Worked examples

For $[1,2,3]$, mean 2 gives deviations $[-1,0,1]$ and squared sum 2. Thus $v_n=2/3$ and $s^2=1$. For $[1,2,2,3,20]$, median 2 gives absolute distances $[1,0,0,1,18]$, whose median is 1. The variance reacts strongly to 20; MAD remains 1.

## Explore

Move the extreme observation while keeping the middle values fixed. Predict which center and spread will change. Add the same constant to every value; then multiply all observations by two. Variances scale by four, standard deviations and MAD by two.

## Common mistakes and counterexamples

A small MAD need not mean every observation is close to the median: a minority of extreme points can coexist with a small central spread. MAD can be zero in a nonconstant discrete dataset when enough values coincide. Robust means resistant to some contamination, not immune to every change.

## Numerical considerations

All supported centered sums and rational variance identities are exact. Square roots and drawing coordinates use binary64; exact root forms are displayed when supported. Raw second-moment subtraction is dangerous for large nearly equal floating values; the teaching model centers exact fractions and bounds inputs. Unscaled MAD is not automatically a Gaussian standard-deviation estimate.

## Engineering and ML connections

Feature scaling and uncertainty estimates need a stated spread convention. Standard deviation measures squared-error scale, while median/MAD can help diagnose contaminated measurements. The factor 1.4826 sometimes applied to MAD assumes a normal calibration; this explorer deliberately uses the unscaled descriptive quantity.
