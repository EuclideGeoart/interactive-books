## Intuition

The observed y values need not lie on a line. A fitted prediction lives on the line at each observed x; the vertical gap is a signed residual. Least squares chooses a line that minimizes the total squared vertical gaps, not orthogonal distances to the line.

## Definition

For $\hat y_i=a+bx_i$, residuals are $r_i=y_i-\hat y_i$ and $\operatorname{RSS}(a,b)=\sum_i r_i^2$. Simple regression here includes an intercept and uses equal observation weights. If $S_{xx}=\sum_i(x_i-\bar x)^2>0$, coefficients are unique. Correlation summarizes standardized linear association; RSS is an unstandardized fit criterion.

## Mechanics

Select and move any observation using the same input paths. The plot shows the actual pairs, derived line and residual segments; the table lists exact predictions and signed residuals. Compare the canonical noisy dataset with a noiseless line, an outlier and a dataset with identical x coordinates.

## Derivation

Setting the RSS derivatives to zero gives $\sum_i r_i=0$ and $\sum_i x_i r_i=0$. Solve them to obtain $b=S_{xy}/S_{xx}$ and $a=\bar y-b\bar x$. In the earlier projection language, the design matrix has columns of ones and observed x values; fitted y is the projection onto their span, and residuals are orthogonal to both columns.

## Worked examples

For x=[−3,−1,0,1,3], y=[−1,0,1,2,3], means are (0,1), $S_{xx}=20$ and $S_{xy}=14$. Thus $b=7/10$ and $a=1$. Residuals are $[1/10,-3/10,0,3/10,-1/10]$, with $\operatorname{RSS}=1/5$. At x=3, prediction is 31/10 and the observed residual is −1/10.

## Common mistakes and counterexamples

When all x values equal x₀, only a+b x₀ equals the mean y; slope and intercept are not individually identified. Fitted values at observed x and RSS remain meaningful, but a unique extrapolation line must not be invented. Low training RSS does not establish causation or guarantee reliable future predictions. Zero y variance makes correlation undefined even when a horizontal regression line is uniquely fitted.

## Numerical considerations

Supported coordinates are bounded decimals represented exactly, so coefficients, predictions and residual identities use fractions. Drawing positions convert to binary64. Exact zero Sxx determines identifiability. Normal equations explain the geometry, but floating numerical software should normally use QR or SVD rather than explicitly invert the Gram matrix; the NumPy reference uses lstsq and exposes rank.

## Engineering and ML connections

Least squares connects a statistical fit with the projection material from linear algebra. Prediction uncertainty requires assumptions about future noise and coefficient estimation; this descriptive fit does not manufacture a predictive interval. A useful model also needs validation beyond the training observations and a justified scope for extrapolation.
