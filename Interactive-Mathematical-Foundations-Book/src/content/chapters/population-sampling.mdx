## Intuition

A statistic changes when a new sample arrives even though the population stays fixed. Random variation concerns which eligible members happen to be selected. Selection bias changes eligibility itself. Repeating a biased procedure can make its wrong center increasingly stable.

## Definition

The population is the fixed multiset $[0,1,2,3,6,7,8,9]$, with mean $\mu=9/2$ and variance $41/4$. Uniform sampling here means iid draws with replacement, each member having probability $1/8$. The biased rule draws uniformly only from $[6,7,8,9]$, whose mean is $15/2$.

## Derivation

Under uniform iid selection, $E[\bar X]=\mu$ and $\operatorname{Var}(\bar X)=\sigma^2/n=41/(4n)$. Under the biased rule, $E[\bar X]=15/2$, so bias relative to the population mean is 3 at every sample size. Its selected-source variance is $5/4$, giving mean variance $5/(4n)$.

## Worked examples

Seed 42 with four uniform draws produces $[2,0,6,1]$, whose mean is $9/4$. This estimate differs from the true finite-population mean $9/2$ without implying selection bias. A sample consisting only of upper-half values instead comes from a procedure with theoretical center $15/2$.

## Common mistakes and counterexamples

An unlucky representative procedure can produce an unrepresentative realized sample. A very large biased sample can still miss a whole subgroup. Sample size alone does not repair exclusion, missingness or dependence. Sampling without replacement has a finite-population correction; this iid explorer does not silently apply that different model.

## Numerical considerations

The bounded LCG uses exact uint32 transitions; integer indexing selects population members without floating accumulation. Sizes are 1–32 and repetitions 1–64. Sample means and empirical summaries are exact fractions. Seeded pseudo-randomness supports reproducibility; it is not evidence that a real collection procedure meets iid assumptions.

## Engineering and ML connections

A monitoring dashboard that records only successful requests misses failures; collecting more successes cannot repair that exclusion. A training dataset assembled from one customer segment can concentrate around a segment-specific distribution. Evaluate the sampling frame as carefully as the number of rows.
